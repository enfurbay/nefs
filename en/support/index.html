Support

Write to us: enfurbay@gmail.com
When reporting a problem, just include the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and a short description of what you did. In the app, Settings › About › Report a bug fills this in for you.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › your Apple Account (your name) › Subscriptions › Nefs. In the app, Settings › Premium › Manage subscription opens the same page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

I bought Premium but it isn't active / I switched phones

In the app, tap Settings › Premium › Restore purchases. If the purchase was made with the same Apple Account, Premium comes back. Purchases waiting for a parent's approval in Family Sharing unlock automatically once approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I'm changing phones. What happens to my entries?

There are two ways: (1) With Premium, sign in with Apple in Settings › Account and sync; your entries come to your new phone through your own iCloud account. (2) Use Settings › Data › Export data (JSON) to get a file, then open it on the new phone with Restore from backup.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Data › Delete all data. If sync is on, you can choose to delete everywhere or only on this device. For your iCloud data, use Settings › Account and sync › Delete account and iCloud data.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are on in iOS Settings › Notifications › Nefs, and that the notification types and times are on in the app's Settings › Notifications. Focus (Do Not Disturb) can delay notifications. On a day you've already finished a step, its reminder is intentionally not sent.

Apple Watch

The watch app comes with Nefs on iPhone; if it isn't on your watch, install it from the Watch app on your iPhone. Entries made on the watch go to your iPhone; if the iPhone is out of reach they wait in a queue and are delivered when the connection returns. One-tap logging requires choosing a main weakness on the iPhone first.

Logging from the watch and the watch face complication are unlocked with Premium; "Struggling" on the watch is open to everyone. If the complication shows a lock, open Nefs on your iPhone once so the Premium status reaches the watch.

iCloud sync

Sync works with Premium and is turned on by signing in with Apple in Settings › Account and sync. If your subscription ends, sync stops but no entries are deleted; they stay on your device and sync resumes where it left off if you subscribe again. Without a subscription you can still move to a new phone with Export data (JSON).
