Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Developer: EFB Apps · Contact: enfurbay@gmail.com

Nefs is a personal self-discipline journal. What you write belongs to you alone. This page describes what the app actually does.

In short

  • No ads, tracking or analytics tools.
  • Your entries stay on your phone (and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account). We have no server and can't see them.
  • No account is required.

What stays on your device

Your slip and held-firm entries, morning pledges, day's end answers, notes, tasks, your own "why", your "if… then…" plan, your "what helped" notes and your settings are stored in the app's database on your phone. They are not sent to us, to advertisers or to analytics services.

To recover from data corruption, the app keeps up to three automatic backups on your device. They never leave the device, and "Delete all data" deletes them too.

The hard-time alert is calculated only on your phone, from the times of your slip entries. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; their text never includes what you wrote.

Widget, Apple Watch and iPad

The widget and the watch face complication show only numbers (days in a row, today's entry counts, the status of the morning pledge and day's end, the last seven days) and the level name. If the app lock is on, these numbers are hidden.

Entries you make on Apple Watch go directly to your own iPhone through Apple's device-to-device connection (WatchConnectivity). The watch keeps no database of its own, and nothing is sent to us or to any server. Your notes, day's end answers and your own "why" are not sent to the watch.

On iPad the app works the same way as on iPhone; data stays on that device.

Optional: Apple Health

If you turn it on in Settings, the breathing time from the "I'm struggling right now" screen is saved to your Apple Health app as a mindful session. This setting is off by default. Nefs reads no data from Health; the entry it writes stays in your Health app only and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can remove the permission anytime in the Health app.

Optional: Sign in with Apple and iCloud sync

If you sign in with Apple in Settings, your entries sync across devices on the same Apple Account.

  • Your name and email address are not requested. The anonymous identifier Apple provides for this app is stored only in your device's keychain.
  • Your entries are stored in your own iCloud account, in a folder reserved for this app that does not appear in Files. We have no server and cannot see this data.
  • The content of an entry you delete is not kept in iCloud. Only its identifier and deletion time remain, so it can also be deleted on your other devices.
  • Settings › Account and sync › Delete account and iCloud data deletes all your Nefs data in iCloud and signs you out. If you remove Sign in with Apple in iOS Settings, the app signs out as well.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store; Apple processes your payment details and we cannot see them. Your subscription is verified through the App Store records on your device (Apple's StoreKit).

If the RevenueCat service is enabled for subscription verification, RevenueCat is used only when you open the payment screen, restore purchases, or when your device already has a purchase for this app. It then processes your purchase history (product, date, status) under an anonymous identifier; it does not receive your name, email or anything you write. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy

Face ID

Face ID is used only for the optional app lock. The app cannot access your face data; iOS performs the verification.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings › Data › Export data (JSON). The file contains all your entries and settings; you choose where to save it.
  • Delete from this device: Settings › Data › Delete all data. The database, automatic backups, widget summary, pending quick entries and notifications are deleted. If sync is on, you choose between deleting everywhere or only on this device.
  • Delete from iCloud: "Delete account and iCloud data" above.
  • Your purchase records remain with Apple (and with RevenueCat, if used). To request their deletion, write to enfurbay@gmail.com.

Children

Nefs is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page and mentioned in the app update.
